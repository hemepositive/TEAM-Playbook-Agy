## TEAM Model — Five Surgical Episode Categories

| # | Procedure | MS-DRG Range | Tier Structure |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lower Extremity Joint Replacement (LEJR) | 469, 470, 521 | MCC / None (2-tier) |
| 2 | Surgical Hip & Femur Fracture Treatment (SHFFT) | 480, 481, 482 | MCC / CC / None (3-tier) |
| 3 | Spinal Fusion | 402, 426–430, 447–451, 471–473 | MCC / CC / None (varies by type) |
| 4 | Coronary Artery Bypass Graft (CABG) | 231–236 | MCC / None (2-tier) |
| 5 | Major Bowel Procedure | 329, 330, 331 | MCC / CC / None (3-tier) |

---

## ⚠️ Key CDI Teaching Points by Tier Structure

- **2-Tier DRGs (LEJR, CABG):** A CC alone does NOT change the DRG. Only an MCC moves reimbursement. This is a high-value teaching moment — capturing a CC in these cases improves SOI/ROM but does not affect the DRG payment.
- **3-Tier DRGs (SHFFT, Major Bowel, most Spinal Fusion):** Both CC and MCC matter. A CC moves from the base DRG; an MCC moves to the highest tier.
- **Spinal Fusion:** Most granular family — level (single vs. multi), approach (combined ant/post vs. single), and region (cervical vs. non-cervical) all determine which DRG family applies BEFORE CC/MCC is considered.

## POA (Present on Admission) Rules — Summary for Game Logic

- A diagnosis is **POA = Yes** if the condition was present at the time of admission
- A diagnosis is **POA = No** if it developed AFTER admission (hospital-acquired condition)
- **HAC (Hospital-Acquired Condition):** If a CC or MCC is POA = No AND it is on the HAC list, it may be **excluded from DRG calculation** — this can reduce reimbursement AND trigger quality penalties
- Common HACs relevant to TEAM procedures:
  - Surgical site infections (T81.4x)
  - Catheter-associated UTI (T83.51xA)
  - Pressure injuries (L89.xx) — Stage 3 and 4
  - Deep vein thrombosis/PE (I82.4x, I26.xx) — post-procedure
  - Falls and trauma (W18.xx)
