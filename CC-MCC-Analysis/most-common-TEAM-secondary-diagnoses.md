Based on clinical coding guidelines and national Medicare claims data (such as the MedPAR and HCUP inpatient databases), here are the **top 10 most commonly encountered Complications or Comorbidities (CCs) and Major Complications or Comorbidities (MCCs)** for each of the five Transforming Episode-Based Payment (TEAM) procedures:

---

### 🦴 1. Lower Extremity Joint Replacement (LEJR)
*LEJR is a 2-tier severity DRG. Capturing a CC does not shift the DRG from 470 to 469 (or 522 to 521). Only an **MCC** will shift the severity tier for reimbursement.*

#### **Top 10 MCCs (Shift Case to 469/521)**
1. **A41.9** – Sepsis, unspecified organism (critical for joint infections/systemic sepsis)
2. **J96.01** – Acute respiratory failure with hypoxia (post-operative respiratory failure)
3. **N17.9** – Acute kidney failure, unspecified (acute kidney injury post-op)
4. **E43** – Unspecified severe protein-calorie malnutrition
5. **G93.41** – Metabolic encephalopathy (post-op delirium/altered mental status)
6. **I50.23** – Acute on chronic systolic heart failure
7. **I50.33** – Acute on chronic diastolic heart failure
8. **I21.4** – Non-ST elevation myocardial infarction (NSTEMI)
9. **J96.21** – Acute on chronic respiratory failure with hypoxia
10. **T84.50XA** – Infection and inflammatory reaction due to unspecified internal joint prosthesis, initial encounter

#### **Top 10 CCs (Do NOT shift DRG, but track SOI/ROM)**
1. **D62** – Acute posthemorrhagic anemia (blood loss drop in hemoglobin requiring monitoring or transfusion)
2. **I48.91** – Unspecified atrial fibrillation (post-op onset or history)
3. **N18.30** – Chronic kidney disease, stage 3 unspecified (baseline renal function)
4. **E11.65** – Type 2 diabetes mellitus with hyperglycemia
5. **E46** – Unspecified protein-calorie malnutrition (moderate malnutrition)
6. **E87.1** – Hypo-osmolality and hyponatremia (electrolyte imbalance)
7. **E87.2** – Acidosis
8. **I12.9** – Hypertensive chronic kidney disease with stage 1-4 CKD
9. **T84.018A** – Broken internal joint prosthesis, other joint, initial encounter (mechanical failure)
10. **N39.0** – Urinary tract infection, site not specified (post-op catheter UTI)

---

### 🦿 2. Surgical Hip & Femur Fracture Treatment (SHFFT)
*SHFFT is a 3-tier severity DRG. Both **CCs** (shifts 482 to 481) and **MCCs** (shifts to 480) drive payment tier adjustments.*

#### **Top 10 MCCs (Shift Case to 480)**
1. **G93.41** – Metabolic encephalopathy (elderly post-fracture/post-op delirium)
2. **E43** – Unspecified severe protein-calorie malnutrition
3. **J96.01** – Acute respiratory failure with hypoxia
4. **A41.9** – Sepsis, unspecified organism
5. **N17.9** – Acute kidney failure, unspecified (AKI due to dehydration/blood loss)
6. **I50.23** – Acute on chronic systolic heart failure
7. **I50.33** – Acute on chronic diastolic heart failure
8. **F02.80** – Dementia in other diseases without behavioral disturbance
9. **G30.9** – Alzheimer's disease, unspecified (contributing to fall/admission complexity)
10. **I21.4** – Non-ST elevation (NSTEMI) myocardial infarction

#### **Top 10 CCs (Shift Case from 482 to 481)**
1. **D62** – Acute posthemorrhagic anemia (due to fracture hematoma and surgery)
2. **E87.1** – Hypo-osmolality and hyponatremia (very common electrolyte imbalance in elderly fall patients)
3. **I48.91** – Unspecified atrial fibrillation
4. **E46** – Unspecified protein-calorie malnutrition (moderate malnutrition)
5. **N18.30** – Chronic kidney disease, stage 3 unspecified
6. **E87.2** – Acidosis
7. **F03.90** – Unspecified dementia without behavioral disturbance
8. **M81.0** – Age-related osteoporosis without current pathological fracture (baseline bone status)
9. **I12.9** – Hypertensive chronic kidney disease
10. **D64.9** – Anemia, unspecified (baseline chronic anemia)

### ❤️ 3. Coronary Artery Bypass Graft (CABG)
*CABG is a 2-tier severity DRG family. Only **MCCs** (shifts to 231, 233, or 235) will adjust the DRG tier.*

#### **Top 10 MCCs (Shift Case to 231, 233, or 235)**
1. **I21.4** – Non-ST elevation (NSTEMI) myocardial infarction (active MI leading to bypass)
2. **J96.01** – Acute respiratory failure with hypoxia (post-pump prolonged ventilation or ARDS)
3. **N17.0** – Acute kidney failure with tubular necrosis (post-pump acute tubular necrosis)
4. **I50.21** – Acute systolic heart failure (cardiogenic shock post-op)
5. **I50.23** – Acute on chronic systolic heart failure
6. **I50.31** – Acute diastolic heart failure
7. **I50.33** – Acute on chronic diastolic heart failure
8. **I27.20** – Pulmonary hypertension, unspecified (severe pre-operative pulmonary hypertension)
9. **D65** – Disseminated intravascular coagulation (post-bypass bleeding coagulopathy)
10. **A41.9** – Sepsis, unspecified organism (mediastinitis or systemic sepsis post-op)

#### **Top 10 CCs (Do NOT shift DRG, but track SOI/ROM)**
1. **I48.11** – Longstanding persistent atrial fibrillation (post-operative Afib is highly common)
2. **E11.65** – Type 2 diabetes with hyperglycemia
3. **N18.30** – Chronic kidney disease, stage 3 unspecified (baseline kidney function)
4. **N18.4** – Chronic kidney disease, stage 4 (severe baseline impairment)
5. **D62** – Acute posthemorrhagic anemia (post-op blood loss)
6. **I48.91** – Unspecified atrial fibrillation
7. **J44.1** – COPD with acute exacerbation
8. **E46** – Unspecified protein-calorie malnutrition (moderate)
9. **I25.110** – Atherosclerotic heart disease of native coronary artery with unstable angina pectoris
10. **E87.1** – Hyponatremia

### 🧫 4. Major Bowel Procedure
*Major Bowel Procedures are 3-tier severity DRGs. Both **CCs** (shifts 331 to 330) and **MCCs** (shifts to 329) drive payment tier adjustments.*

#### **Top 10 MCCs (Shift Case to 329)**
1. **A41.9** – Sepsis, unspecified organism (abdominal sepsis/peritonitis from leak or perforation)
2. **R65.21** – Severe sepsis with septic shock (requiring vasopressors)
3. **K63.1** – Perforation of intestine (non-traumatic) (primary indicator leading to emergent surgery)
4. **E43** – Unspecified severe protein-calorie malnutrition (common in bowel obstruction/cancer wasting)
5. **J96.01** – Acute respiratory failure with hypoxia (post-operative respiratory failure)
6. **N17.9** – Acute kidney failure, unspecified (third-spacing/severe dehydration post-op)
7. **C18.9** – Malignant neoplasm of colon, unspecified (secondary diagnosis complicating case)
8. **K56.609** – Unspecified intestinal obstruction
9. **K55.021** – Acute infarction of small intestine (ischemic bowel)
10. **G93.41** – Metabolic encephalopathy (post-operative toxic delirium)

#### **Top 10 CCs (Shift Case from 331 to 330)**
1. **Z93.3** – Colostomy status (temporary/permanent ostomy creation or history)
2. **Z93.2** – Ileostomy status (ostomy creation/history)
3. **D62** – Acute posthemorrhagic anemia (GI bleed or surgical blood loss)
4. **E46** – Unspecified protein-calorie malnutrition (moderate malnutrition)
5. **E87.1** – Hyponatremia (electrolyte depletion from prep/vomiting)
6. **K56.699** – Other intestinal obstruction
7. **K57.32** – Diverticulitis of large intestine without perforation or abscess without hemorrhage
8. **N18.30** – Chronic kidney disease, stage 3 unspecified
9. **E87.2** – Acidosis
10. **K52.9** – Noninfective gastroenteritis and colitis, unspecified

### 🧠 5. Spinal Fusion
*Spinal Fusion is a complex family containing both 2-tier and 3-tier severity structures. Documenting CCs and MCCs refines case classification within specific surgical approaches.*

#### **Top 10 MCCs (Shift Case Severity Category)**
1. **M46.20** – Osteomyelitis of vertebra, site unspecified (spinal infection necessitating surgical fusion)
2. **J96.01** – Acute respiratory failure with hypoxia (common in high cervical fusions)
3. **E43** – Unspecified severe protein-calorie malnutrition (frail chronic pain patients)
4. **G93.41** – Metabolic encephalopathy (altered mental status due to opioid/anesthesia combinations)
5. **A41.9** – Sepsis, unspecified organism
6. **N17.9** – Acute kidney failure, unspecified
7. **G35** – Multiple sclerosis (pre-existing clinical complication)
8. **I50.23** – Acute on chronic systolic heart failure
9. **G82.20** – Paraplegia, unspecified (spinal cord injury)
10. **G82.50** – Quadriplegia, unspecified

#### **Top 10 CCs (Shift Case Severity Category)**
1. **D62** – Acute posthemorrhagic anemia (major blood loss during multi-level fusions)
2. **F11.20** – Opioid dependence, uncomplicated (chronic opioid therapy for back pain)
3. **N18.30** – Chronic kidney disease, stage 3 unspecified
4. **E87.1** – Hyponatremia (common post-operative fluid shifts)
5. **E11.65** – Type 2 diabetes with hyperglycemia
6. **E46** – Unspecified protein-calorie malnutrition (moderate)
7. **I48.91** – Unspecified atrial fibrillation
8. **F17.210** – Nicotine dependence, cigarettes, uncomplicated (critical to document as smoking directly affects bone fusion union success)
9. **M81.0** – Age-related osteoporosis without current pathological fracture
10. **N39.0** – Urinary tract infection, site not specified (post-op catheter UTI)
