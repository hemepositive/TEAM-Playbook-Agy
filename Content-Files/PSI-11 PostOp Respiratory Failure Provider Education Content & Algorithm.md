# **PSI 11: Clinical Accuracy & Documentation Guide**

### **High-Value Care for Physicians and Providers**

---

**\[SHADED BOX START\]**

### **Why This Matters**

**Patient Safety Indicator 11 (PSI 11\)** is a primary metric used by CMS to assess surgical quality. Because it relies on **administrative coding**, your specific word choice in the EMR determines whether a patient's course is flagged as a "surgical complication" or accurately reflected as "routine clinical management."

* **Impact:** Influences CMS reimbursement, hospital star ratings, and public Leapfrog/U.S. News rankings.  
  **\[SHADED BOX END\]**

---

## **1\. What Triggers a PSI 11 Flag?**

A case is flagged as a "Postoperative Respiratory Failure" complication if any of the following occur after an **elective** surgery:

* **The Diagnosis:** "Acute postoperative respiratory failure" is documented as a secondary diagnosis.  
* **The 96-Hour Rule:** Mechanical ventilation lasts \> 96 hours starting on/after the procedure.  
* **The 24-Hour Rule:** Ventilation for 24–96 hours that starts 2+ days after surgery.  
* **Reintubation:** Any unplanned intubation occurring 1 or more days after surgery.

---

## **2\. The Documentation Decision Algorithm**

Before documenting "Respiratory Failure," apply these three filters:

1. **Indication: Protection vs. Failure?**  
   * If the patient remains intubated for **Airway Protection** (e.g., risk of edema, combativeness, or anticipated return to OR), it is not a failure.  
   * *Action:* Document: *"Prolonged mechanical ventilation for airway protection; no intrinsic pulmonary failure."*  
2. **Intent: Planned vs. Unplanned?**  
   * If the ventilation was part of a staged procedure or a high-risk surgical protocol.  
   * *Action:* Document: *"Planned postoperative ventilation per surgical protocol."*  
3. **Causality: Surgical vs. Medical?**  
   * Is the hypoxia due to a surgical error, or is it a progression of **Chronic Disease** (COPD, OSA, Heart Failure)?  
   * *Action:* Document the specific medical cause: *"Acute on chronic hypoxic respiratory failure due to COPD exacerbation."*

## **3\. Provider "Cheat Sheet": Dos and Don'ts**

**\[TWO-COLUMN LAYOUT START\]**

**AVOID (Triggers Flag)** 🚩

* "Postoperative Respiratory Failure"  
* "Post-op Hypoxia" (without cause)  
* "Respiratory Failure" (if only using 2L O2)

**USE (Clinically Accurate)** ✅

* "Acute on Chronic \[Type\] Resp Failure"  
* "Acute Pulmonary Insufficiency"  
* "Ventilation for Airway Protection"  
* "Acute Hypoxia secondary to \[Pneumonia/CHF\]"  
  **\[TWO-COLUMN LAYOUT END\]**

---

### **Exclusions to Remember**

PSI 11 does **not** apply if the patient has:

* Neuromuscular disorders (e.g., Myasthenia Gravis).  
* Degenerative brain disorders.  
* Tracheostomy present on admission.  
* Specific head/neck or esophageal surgeries (high-risk airway zones).
