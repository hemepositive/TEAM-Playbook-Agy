# TEAM Quick Reference — Spinal Fusion

**DRGs:** 13 families across cervical, thoracic, and lumbar regions; anterior and posterior approaches
**Tier structure:** Variable — some families are 2-tier (MCC only), others are 3-tier (CC and MCC). **Know your DRG family before assuming CCs have payment value.**
**~Payment delta (3-tier families) MCC → CC:** ~$4,000–$6,000 · **CC → base:** ~$2,500–$4,000
**~Payment delta (2-tier families) MCC → base:** ~$6,000–$9,000 (CCs have no payment impact)

> [NOTE] DRG 402 (Cervical Spinal Fusion without CC/MCC) has no MCC/CC tier — all cases pay the same regardless of comorbidities. For all other spinal fusion DRG families, MCC documentation is high value.

## Top 5 MCCs — Highest Payment Impact

| # | MCC | Why It's Missed | ICD-10 |
|---|-----|-----------------|--------|
| 1 | **Acute Kidney Injury** | Creatinine elevation described as lab value, not diagnosis | N17.0–N17.9 |
| 2 | **Metabolic or Toxic Encephalopathy** | "Confusion" or "altered mental status" documented — neither is codeable as encephalopathy | G93.41, G92.9 |
| 3 | **Severe Protein-Calorie Malnutrition** | Dietitian assessment not followed by physician diagnosis | E43 |
| 4 | **Obesity Hypoventilation Syndrome** | "Morbid obesity on CPAP" or "OSA" documented instead | E66.2 |
| 5 | **Acute Respiratory Failure** | "Hypoxia" or "respiratory distress" documented — symptoms, not the diagnosis | J96.01, J96.02 |

**Also consider:** Sepsis (A41.x + R65.20), acute systolic/diastolic HF (I50.21–I50.43), vertebral osteomyelitis when fusion is for infectious indication (M46.2x)

## Key HCCs — Target Price Risk Adjustment

Spinal fusion has 13 HCC risk adjustment variables. The population skews younger than LEJR/SHFFT but carries significant metabolic and psychiatric comorbidity.

| HCC | Condition | Documentation Trigger |
|-----|-----------|----------------------|
| 72 | Spinal cord disorder / myelopathy | "Cervical myelopathy" not "cervical stenosis with weakness" — the diagnosis, not the symptom |
| 155/158 | Major depression (moderate-severe / mild) | Highly prevalent in chronic pain population; name it, specify severity |
| 37 | Diabetes with chronic complications | Name the complication — neuropathy is especially relevant for spine patients |
| 48 | Morbid obesity | BMI ≥40 with physician diagnosis; OHS (E66.2) separately if present |
| 54 | Drug/alcohol dependence | Active substance use disorder — not "history of" if currently active |
| 280 | COPD | Severity and exacerbation status at every encounter |
| 21 | Bone/joint infection | Vertebral osteomyelitis when fusion performed for infectious indication |

## Exact Phrasing Examples

**Myelopathy (spinal fusion-specific)**
Instead of: *"Cervical stenosis with upper extremity weakness — proceed with ACDF."*
Write: *"Cervical spondylotic myelopathy at C5-C6 (G99.2), bilateral upper extremity weakness and impaired fine motor function, modified JOA score 12/18. Proceeding with anterior cervical discectomy and fusion."*

**Morbid Obesity**
Instead of: *"Obese, BMI 42.5."*
Write: *"Morbid obesity (BMI 42.5) — patient's severe obesity requires specialized surgical positioning, customized bariatric bed in recovery, and extra personnel for mobilization."*

**OHS vs. OSA**
Instead of: *"Morbid obesity, uses CPAP at home for sleep apnea."*
Write: *"Obesity hypoventilation syndrome (E66.2) — BMI 46, home BiPAP, prior ABG with pCO₂ 52 mmHg. Daytime hypercapnia consistent with OHS rather than OSA alone. BiPAP continued perioperatively."*

**Depression in chronic pain patient**
Instead of: *"Patient anxious about surgery, on antidepressant."*
Write: *"Major depressive disorder, moderate (F33.1), on escitalopram 20mg with partial response. Active diagnosis, being managed by PCP."*

**AKI after spinal fusion**
Instead of: *"Creatinine up to 1.9 from baseline 1.0 — likely related to NSAIDs, monitoring."*
Write: *"Acute kidney injury stage 2 (KDIGO criteria — creatinine 1.9× baseline), likely NSAID and contrast-related. NSAIDs held, IV hydration initiated, urine output monitoring in place."*

**Malnutrition in spine patient**
Instead of: *"Patient has poor nutritional status, dietitian following."*
Write: *"Moderate protein-calorie malnutrition (E44.0) — unintentional 8% weight loss over 3 months in setting of chronic pain limiting activity and appetite, muscle wasting on examination. Consistent with ASPEN criteria for moderate malnutrition in chronic illness context."*

## Discharge Documentation Checklist

**Before signing the discharge summary, confirm:**

- [ ] **Myelopathy** — if fusion performed for neurological indication: document myelopathy as diagnosis (not just stenosis); specify level and neurological findings
- [ ] **AKI** — if creatinine rose at any point: document AKI with stage and etiology (NSAID, contrast, hypovolemia)
- [ ] **OHS** — if patient uses home BiPAP or has documented daytime hypercapnia: document OHS (E66.2), not just morbid obesity or OSA
- [ ] **Encephalopathy** — if acute confusion occurred: name metabolic or toxic etiology; distinguish from delirium
- [ ] **Malnutrition** — if nutrition consult placed or supplemental nutrition initiated: physician documents severity
- [ ] **Depression** — if patient on antidepressant for MDD (not just pain): document MDD with current severity
- [ ] **Diabetes** — specify type and complications; neuropathy is especially relevant in spine patients
- [ ] **COPD** — if respiratory involvement during hospitalization: document exacerbation status
- [ ] **Substance use** — if withdrawal managed during stay: document withdrawal diagnosis, not just "history of use"
- [ ] **DRG family check** — confirm your procedure maps to a 3-tier DRG family before assuming CCs carry payment weight; check the DRG family navigator in the full procedure module
- [ ] **Anemia** — if transfusion given: document acute blood loss anemia (D62)
- [ ] **Morbid Obesity** — if BMI is ≥40, or BMI is 35–39.9 with severe weight-exacerbated comorbidities: document "morbid obesity" to capture CC (+1.4214 weight shift on 3-tier cases) and HCC 48
- [ ] **POA status** — all secondary diagnoses should reflect whether present on admission
