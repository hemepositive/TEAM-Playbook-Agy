# TEAM Quick Reference — Lower Extremity Joint Replacement

**DRGs:** 469 (with MCC) · 470 (without CC/MCC) · 521 (hip fracture, with MCC) · 522 (hip fracture, without MCC)
**Tier structure:** 2-tier — **only MCCs move payment**. CCs have no DRG payment impact in LEJR. Document CCs for SOI/ROM quality metrics only.
**~Payment delta MCC → base:** ~$4,500–$6,000 per case (national average; varies by wage index)

---

<!-- Section: TopMCCs -->
## Top 5 MCCs — Highest Payment Impact

| # | MCC | Why It's Missed | ICD-10 |
|---|-----|-----------------|--------|
| 1 | **Acute Kidney Injury** (any stage) | Creatinine rises documented as lab values, not diagnoses | N17.0–N17.9 |
| 2 | **Acute Systolic or Diastolic Heart Failure** | "CHF" or "heart failure" written instead — neither is an MCC | I50.21, I50.23, I50.31, I50.33 |
| 3 | **Metabolic or Toxic Encephalopathy** | "Confusion" or "AMS" documented — neither is codeable as encephalopathy | G93.41, G92.9 |
| 4 | **Severe Protein-Calorie Malnutrition** | Dietitian documents it; physician never does — coder cannot use dietitian-only diagnosis | E43 |
| 5 | **Obesity Hypoventilation Syndrome** | "Morbid obesity" or "OSA" written instead of OHS — different conditions, different codes | E66.2 |

**Also consider:** Acute respiratory failure (J96.01), sepsis (A41.x + R65.20/R65.21), acute pulmonary edema (J81.0), acute MI (I21.x)

## Key HCCs — Target Price Risk Adjustment

HCCs are documented in the **180 days before surgery** and adjust the episode target price — not the DRG. Document these at every pre-op encounter.

| HCC | Condition | Documentation Trigger |
|-----|-----------|----------------------|
| 224/225/226 | Heart failure (any type) | Specify systolic vs. diastolic; acute vs. chronic |
| 238 | Atrial fibrillation | Specify type — paroxysmal, persistent, permanent |
| 280 | COPD | Document severity and current exacerbation status |
| 37 | Diabetes with chronic complications | Name the complication — neuropathy, nephropathy, retinopathy |
| 327 | CKD Stage 4 | Stage the CKD explicitly; "CKD" alone is insufficient |
| 48 | Morbid obesity | BMI ≥40 with physician diagnosis; OHS adds E66.2 |
| 125/126/127 | Dementia (any severity) | Name it, stage it — "confusion" does not capture the HCC |

**Annual re-documentation required.** "History of CHF" does not count. Must document as active with management plan.

<!-- Section: PhrasingExamples -->
## Exact Phrasing Examples

**AKI**
Instead of: *"Creatinine 2.4, slightly elevated, monitoring."*
Write: *"Acute kidney injury, stage 2 by KDIGO criteria, likely prerenal. Managed with IV fluid resuscitation, nephrology consulted, nephrotoxins held."*

**Heart failure**
Instead of: *"CHF — continue home diuretics."*
Write: *"Acute on chronic systolic heart failure (EF 35%). IV Lasix given with 2L net negative output. Transitioning to oral diuresis."*

**Encephalopathy**
Instead of: *"Patient confused postop, likely multifactorial."*
Write: *"Metabolic encephalopathy secondary to uremia and electrolyte disturbance. CAM positive. Reorientation protocol initiated, underlying metabolic cause being treated."*

**Malnutrition**
Instead of: *"Poor appetite, dietary consult placed."*
Write: *"Moderate protein-calorie malnutrition per ASPEN clinical criteria — significant weight loss, reduced intake, muscle wasting on exam. Nutrition support initiated."*

**OHS**
Instead of: *"Morbid obesity, on CPAP at home."*
Write: *"Obesity hypoventilation syndrome (BMI 44, home BiPAP, baseline pCO₂ elevated). CPAP/BiPAP continued in-house."*

<!-- Section: DischargeChecklist -->
## Discharge Documentation Checklist

**Before signing the discharge summary, confirm:**

- [ ] **AKI** — if creatinine rose ≥0.3 mg/dL or ≥1.5× baseline at any point: document AKI with stage and etiology
- [ ] **Heart failure** — if IV diuresis given, BNP elevated, or volume management required: specify systolic vs. diastolic, acute vs. chronic
- [ ] **Encephalopathy** — if CAM positive, acute confusion, or altered baseline cognition: name the type and etiology (metabolic, toxic, etc.)
- [ ] **Malnutrition** — if nutrition consult placed or supplemental nutrition started: document severity as physician diagnosis (mild/moderate/severe)
- [ ] **OHS** — if patient uses home BiPAP or has known hypercapnia: document OHS explicitly, not just "morbid obesity"
- [ ] **Diabetes** — specify type (1 or 2) and any complications (neuropathy, nephropathy, retinopathy); avoid "diabetes mellitus" alone
- [ ] **Anemia** — if transfusion given or Hgb dropped significantly: document acute blood loss anemia (D62), not just "anemia"
- [ ] **Atrial fibrillation** — if AF occurred or continued: document type (paroxysmal, persistent, long-standing persistent)
- [ ] **COPD** — if bronchodilators escalated, respiratory therapy involved, or O₂ requirement changed: document acute exacerbation
- [ ] **Sepsis** — if blood cultures drawn, IV antibiotics started, and clinical picture supports: document sepsis with source; do not leave as "infection" alone
- [ ] **POA status** — confirm all secondary diagnoses reflect whether present on admission or developed during stay
